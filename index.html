How to play

Easy controls, satisfying merges, and charming animals make every round a fresh challenge.

🎯

01. Aim & launch

Choose your direction and launch an animal into the play area using simple touch controls.

🐾

02. Match & merge

Bring matching animals together to create a new animal and keep your chain going.

🏆

03. Beat your score

Plan each move, discover more merges, and see how high you can score.
